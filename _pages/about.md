Welcome to the **V**ision, **I**maging, **P**erception & **S**ecurity Laboratory (VIPS). We conduct research at the intersection of visual perception, computational imaging, and multimedia security. VIPS aims to advance next-generation multimedia perception and build secure, reliable AI systems.

### 🧠 Research Interests
- Computational Modeling of the Human Visual Perception
- Perceptual Multimedia Signal Processing and Quality Assessment
- Multimedia Quality-of-Experience
- Camera Processing Pipeline Design and Optimization

# 🧑‍🎓 Admissions

I recruit 1–2 PhD students and 1–2 Master’s students each year, and I also welcome motivated bachelor students to join the group in any format.
If you are interested in joining VIPS, please feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:zhihua.wang@my.cityu.edu.hk).
